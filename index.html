Poker solitaire in three dimensions.

▶ Play Poker Blocks

Plays right here in your browser. No download, no sign-up.

♠

Build in three dimensions

Your hand is a row of card blocks. Pick one or more and drag them onto a free face of the structure on the table: on top, beside or underneath what is already there. Turn the structure to see every side and find the best place to build.

♥

Make scoring lines

Every straight row of touching blocks, along any of the three directions, scores as a poker hand. The game picks the strongest hand the row can make. A long row scores its best five cards, not every overlap. After each play the score is explained, line by line: the hand, its base value, what changed it and the total.

♦

Shape your strategy

Blocks come in materials, and you can install modifiers on your rack. Each one changes scoring, or what happens on the table, at its own moment, and some only work in the right place or order. Inspect any block or modifier in the game to see exactly what it does.

♣

Reach the target

Each stage gives you a target score and a limited number of plays. Reach it to win the stage, then spend what you earned in the shop to grow your run. Fall short and the run ends.
