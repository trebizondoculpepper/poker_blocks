Music room

Loops from music/loops/. Click a loop: on the next bar it fades in (shape selectable) on the same beat, at the current tempo; the old loop stops once the fade completes, then, after an adjustable delay, the tempo eases to the new loop's own speed like a tape machine.

Record player wind-down and wind-up

Wind down cuts the power: the loop slows to a stop, pitch falling, and goes silent at the end. While wound down, clicking a loop (or Wind up) starts it from a standstill and speeds it up to tempo. The game uses the defaults shown here.

Slowdown curve: 1 slows at a steady rate; higher drops fast at first, then coasts. Silent tail: the last part of the wind-down over which the sound fades out.

Tap tempo

While a loop plays, tap along on every beat with the button or the space bar, starting on beat 1 of a bar. Half speed slows the loop (tape-style) to make tapping easier; the estimate is always the loop's real tempo.
