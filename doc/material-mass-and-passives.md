# Authored block mass and four bounded passives (#1081)

Owner-approved target from #1061. This supersedes the earlier physical calibration and Dense/Sparse/Heavy/Light trial curves, not the composition, snapshot, save or release-status rules. Runtime deliveries are #1067–#1073; artwork #1009 remains separate. No further bench expansion or player study is a prerequisite for implementing these playable trials. They remain provisional until separately certified for ordinary acquisition.

## One physical mass capability

A block represents a nominal **10 cm × 10 cm × 10 cm**, one litre. This authoring volume does not change its rendering dimensions or collision cells. Each material definition declares a validated positive mass recipe: physical component density × authored volume, explicit effective kilograms with rationale, or a registered instance-derived mass provider with explicit configuration. Generic queries dispatch that capability uniformly; they never branch on material or wrapper identity or consult a name-keyed mass table. The table below is documentation for authors, not a runtime lookup.

These are gameplay authoring choices, not measured claims about particular objects. Rounded effective values intentionally replace earlier component estimates. For half-filled Custard/Earth and Gelato, the stated total is authoritative: do not silently add old plastic-shell mass. Keep shared presets in authored configuration; Magic's ratio derives from the Paper calibration rather than from a duplicated UI constant. Changing a material's shader, visual container, animation, camera scale or prototype appearance does not change mass. Appearance-only variants inherit the revised base physical recipe. A genuinely distinct experimental construction may retain its explicit complete recipe and rationale, but every admitted definition must validate and publish mass; do not silently classify variants by their names.

Use the same pure, snapshot-scoped resolved mass for scoring, inspection and rocking. Field total counts placed field-owned instances once; hand/deck/discard/shop and detached visual ghosts do not count. No per-frame live rescans or state diffing to infer actions. Invalidate only with committed snapshot revisions. Empty scope is unavailable for modifier scoring, not zero mass deserving a bonus. Invalid/missing/nonfinite recipes are explicit composition/query errors, not neutral values disguised as valid data.

## Contents-derived mass without revealing contents

The wrapper definition composes an instance-mass provider referencing its existing owned payload capability. That provider reads the immutable stored playable descriptor and resolves its material's registered physical recipe through the same registry. Do not materialize an active card into a live zone, allocate identity, open a wrapper, draw RNG, subscribe to gameplay or mutate the payload to measure it. A wrapper contributes only that resolved number to an aggregate; the stored payload is not counted a second time as another field card.

The owner explicitly superseded fixed wrapping mass and accepted that displayed weight may hint at contents. Ordinary inspection shows **mass only**, never hidden rank/suit/material identity or the payload descriptor. This is a narrow public derived value, not permission for generic inspection to expose the whole hidden object. Current payload validation permits one ownership level and rejects recursive/self-aliased/duplicate descriptors; mass resolution preserves those limits and fails explicitly on an invalid/missing provider. It does not introduce nested Presents. Exact clones retain equal mass through an independently cloned payload; save/restore retains the descriptor and recalculates through the same authored capability. Opening replaces the wrapper with the already stored instance under existing model ownership rules, never rerolls its mass. Shape change, countdown and visual burns do not affect an effective mass recipe.

A material-definition data sheet with no instance states “contents-derived” and explains the rule; a concrete unopened instance always shows its numeric resolved mass. Do not choose a fictional fixed value for definition-only previews or suppress instance weight merely because it is opaque. Broken compositions show an explicit unavailable/error explanation, never fabricated zero grams.

## Inspection and data sheets (#1069)

Register a common mass inspection provider/facet using the shared resolved capability, composed with existing object-description providers. Every material data sheet includes its mass/construction or dynamic rule. Every inspectable material instance includes a **Weight** line in grams or kilograms, regardless of its other descriptions; no host-specific material cases or second mass map. Use a shared formatter (g below 1 kg, kg otherwise, sensible precision up to three significant digits) while retaining full numerical precision in scoring. Examples: Air 1.2 g, Cloud 2 g, Paper 16 g, Magic 48 g, Terracotta 878 g, Gold 19.3 kg. Weight is displayed colloquially; the data and calculations are mass in kg, not force in newtons.

Refresh through the existing live inspection publication when a material/payload/state revision changes, and on restored instance inspection. Views request facts; they do not calculate physical recipes from visual meshes. Object browser, hover, long-press and keyboard inspection share this source. A material without its old optional description still needs the composed mass description; do not rely on today's `inspectBlock` early return to silently omit it.

## Scoring scope, phases and four definitions

All four passives measure **every card currently in the playfield**, from the immutable scoring snapshot after opening and pre-score transformations. They observe context, not deck, hand or discarded history. Use the same snapshot for every row of a scoring pass. Geometry is occupied cells divided by the volume of the smallest grid-axis-aligned box enclosing them, inclusive side lengths `max-min+1`; any packed shape qualifies, including a line. No connected-subset filter or camera-based bounds. One cell is fully packed. No field means unavailable and no modifier activation.

Retain the existing per-scored-row frequency from the trial contract: one contribution per installed copy per actual scored row; not per block or an extra whole-play factor. **Phase decision:** weight multipliers use the existing `score.lineMultiply` phase; packing exponents use `score.linePower`. This reuses the material scoring phase order (multiplication before exponentiation), with normal rack order within the phase and ordinary final integer rounding. A generic scorer dispatches registered effect handlers; add a query-driven exponent handler if missing rather than make the multiplication handler recognize a passive identity. Each outcome records operation, operand, before/after and measurement so narration describes the real result. Zero scores remain zero. Neutral operands add no false “bonus” narration. Independent copies follow ordinary stacking; the configured curve is capped per contribution, not silently a global score ceiling or permission for unbounded parameter upgrades.

Let `clamp(t) = min(1,max(0,t))`. Initial owner-defined configurations:

- **Dense:** `e = 1 + 0.3 × clamp((packing-0.70)/0.30)`; contribute `score^e`. At/below 70% there is no bonus; 80% gives exponent 1.1, 90% 1.2, 100% 1.3. This replaces both ×6 and perfect-only proposals and the old multiplier trial.
- **Sparse:** `e = 1 + 0.3 × clamp((0.20-packing)/0.10)`; contribute `score^e`. At/above 20% there is no bonus; 15% gives 1.15; at/below 10% cap at 1.3. Ten percent is a tuning threshold, not a mathematical minimum.
- **Light:** total field mass `M` kg; `t = clamp((1-M)/0.9)`. Multiplier `1 + 9 × easeOut(t)`, neutral at/above 1 kg, capped ×10 at/below 0.1 kg.
- **Heavy:** reference `G = 19.32 kg`, authored from the shared Gold calibration (display approximately 19.3). `t = clamp((M-G)/(3G))`. Multiplier `1 + 9 × easeOut(t)`, neutral up to `G`, capped ×10 at/above `4G = 77.28 kg`. Store the reference in the passive's configuration from the shared physical preset; generic scoring never looks up a literal Gold identity.

**Ease-out choice:** use registered quadratic ease-out `easeOut(t) = 1-(1-t)^2`. It satisfies the owner's quick early gain followed by a smooth plateau, reaches both finite anchors exactly and has zero derivative at the cap. Midway through either mass interval the factor is 7.75; Light's midpoint is 0.55 kg and Heavy's is 48.3 kg. A linear ramp lacks the requested taper; the old inverse/log curves miss the new neutral/cap anchors. This is a reversible designer choice within the approved easing request, not a physical law. Validate finite ordered endpoints, positive mass span, maximum multiplier 10 and maximum exponent 1.3 on these definitions; no in-game infinite upgrade path is added. General reusable curves may expose other parameters to explicit dev tooling without altering production definitions.

Preserve saved configured trial instances and exports as historical configurations; do not reinterpret an old multiplier as an exponent under the same saved rule meaning. Version the changed configuration/curve descriptors or add new registered profiles, and provide an explicit migration/rejection decision in the implementation rather than mutate an active trial's committed history. Normal new trial scenarios use these four owner-directed profiles, with field-total mass; old subset/mean hypotheses remain clearly historical/optional comparisons, not the defaults. Certification and shop economy are unchanged: playable trials can be used without silently adding provisional passives to ordinary stock.

## Evidence, alternatives and verification limits

`src/physical-mass.ts` supplies component/effective recipes and validation; `src/materials.ts:MaterialDefinition.mass` owns authored data. `src/field-mass.ts:cardsMass/fieldMass` currently reads definition mass and cannot resolve payload-derived instance mass; that is a concrete extension for #1068, not a claim it already works. `src/playable-payload.ts:payloadProblems` validates one stored ownership level and `materializePayload` performs restoration: a pure descriptor mass query must not reuse active adoption as a shortcut. `src/traits/inspectable.ts:inspectionProviders/inspectBlock` supplies live description composition but returns early when the base description is absent, so universal material weight needs explicit composition coverage. `src/tools/object-browser.ts` owns definition metadata presentation, not the physical source of truth.

`src/trial-modifiers.ts:scoreCurves/queryFactorRules` currently provides query-driven multipliers at `score.beforeLineFinalized`, including old heavyLog/lightInverse; `src/field-mass.ts` already exposes whole-field and subset queries. `src/passive-effects.ts:linePhases` orders `score.lineMultiply`, `score.linePower`, `score.beforeLineFinalized`; `src/scoring-modifier.ts` configures ordinary multiply/power handlers by phase. These support the chosen reusable phase wiring and identify what must change. They do not prove the new passives are implemented or balanced. Existing NIST density references in the earlier mass calibration support retained water/gold anchors; hollow/effective and fantasy values above are explicit owner decisions and engineering assumptions.

Required checks: every admitted base/experimental recipe valid; precise totals and g/kg formatting; altered material, restoration and exact clone; payload mass without activation/RNG/identity allocation or content disclosure; missing/invalid payload diagnostics; no wrapper/payload double count; empty scope neutral; field-only scope; inclusive bounds and all shapes; threshold/clamp/midpoint samples; correct multiply/power phase and final rounding; independent copies and all four combined; captured outcomes under later moves; legacy trial semantics; and live inspection across hosts. Supply playable evidence showing the measured field, actual applied operand and awarded score. No new browser benchmark, player-session balance evidence or release certification is claimed by this docs-only design.
