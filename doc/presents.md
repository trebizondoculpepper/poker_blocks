# Presents: opaque playable-card containers and round rewards

**Agreed target, documentation first (#648).** This replaces the earlier shop-based Present proposal, including its price, resale, shop probability and round-eight frequency rules. Those old ticket comments are historical. Present gameplay remains separate implementation work; gift-wrap preview art does not prove reward, container or reveal logic exists.

## Acquisition and lifetime

Winning **stage three of any round** grants exactly one Present. This includes the final configured round, regardless of whether post-game play exists. A lost stage, stage one victory and stage two victory grant none. The stage-three reward is configured on that progression stage through reusable event conditions and reward-grant handlers; it does not depend on a dealer's name, a boss flag or a scene label. Repeated settlement/Continue must not award it again.

A Present is not shop stock, cannot be bought and cannot be sold. Remove the old six-coin price, half-price resale and one-third-of-shops occurrence policy from its active design and implementation briefs. No extra shop slot replaces it. Generic shop and input code use registered offer/sale capabilities; they do not reject a Present by identity. If an old build/save contains an owned Present, keep that valid owned container, but apply the current non-sellable policy. No retroactive round gifts are granted merely by loading an older save. Stock-offer migration must remove obsolete Present offers through a documented compatibility adapter if any have actually shipped; do not invent such offers in new stock.

On settlement the new wrapped instance enters the authoritative run deck once, not the hand or Items rack. It is visible as an unopened Present in inventory, with contents concealed. It remains in the run until opened or explicitly removed. Unopened Presents can be discarded under the ordinary discard capability; they return at the normal round boundary. Multiple rewards may accumulate across rounds; there is no run-wide cap.

Leaving the stage-three shop completes the round's collection/reset sequence. Before the next round's initial deal, shuffle the remaining ordered deck including the new Present and eligible returning cards; draws pop cards from that saved shuffled order. Do not force the Present into the next hand, shuffle just it to the front, or generate a fresh identity at draw time. This aligns with ordered deck work #550/#551; current random-selection-per-draw (`src/deal.ts`) is a migration gap, not a second intended deal policy. This change does not add shuffles between stages one and two or otherwise redefine existing zone/reset rules. At the final stage-three victory, the reward is still committed and recorded without requiring a next stage or inventing post-game flow.

## Creation-fixed hidden contents

A Present is an **opaque container for one playable card**, not a card whose rank/suit are rolled when opened. At creation, the authoritative seeded generator fixes the whole playable-card payload from the upcoming round's shop-card distribution. At the configured final round, use that round's distribution. Resolve this through configured progression/table selection, not a round-eight or Present-name branch.

Use the same distribution policy for material, rank and suit as that table's eligible playable-card offers, without helpful-result bias; duplicate card identities are allowed. Exclude Paper, another wrapped Present and experimental-only results through registered eligible-pool configuration, then normalize eligible weights. Do not sample complete shop stock or install a shop to generate one payload. Empty eligible pools fail the reward transaction without partial grants/RNG changes, not by inventing a fallback material. The next-round distribution is chosen at reward creation even if the Present remains unopened for several rounds; later unlocks or balance changes do not reroll it.

The wrapper is the sole independently drawable live inventory object. Its payload is saved serializable instance state inside that container, not another card in a zone or a second inventory count. Save/restore and exact clone preserve the fixed payload; clones and adopted revealed cards receive collision-free live identities and independent mutable state under the ownership/cloning contract. An exact clone opens to the same rank, suit, material and stored payload state. Public inspection reveals only that it contains an unknown playable card. Seed/debug displays and reward ledger snapshots must not inadvertently disclose it. Reset-clone policy remains the separate facet design; nothing here authorizes rerolling the contents.

## Opening and scoring

Ordinary placement rules apply, including a first placement and selected group. The model immediately opens each placed wrapper in placement order by adopting its stored card at the same cell. All opening completes before pre-score material actions, scoring and post-score material actions. The revealed card participates in the current play under ordinary scoring eligibility and remains a run-owned card. The wrapper no longer exists as an independently owned object. Opening is replacement, not destruction, discard or a second placement action, and consumes no new RNG to choose contents.

The inspectable, discardable, replacement, cloning and ownership capabilities are reusable registered behavior, not Present cases in generic code. Preserve [the gameplay lifecycle](game-events.md), including `block.opened` snapshots and model-first resolution. Visuals may show the wrapper for five game-time units before Perlin reveal, dazzle/confetti/pop and configured whistles, then material/scoring feedback. They never delay model resolution or valid input. Existing reveal celebration design is unchanged.

## Mixed reward ledger and sound

The won-stage ledger supports registered reward entries with typed effects, display values and sound cues. A coin entry changes the financial total; a card grant changes inventory. Do not encode the Present as a zero-coin row or make the generic ledger branch on a reward/item/source identity. A registered grant command creates/adopts the container and records its instance ID; a registered presentation handler formats the non-cash row.

Keep existing cash rows and their order; append a **Present added** row after the cash rows on stage-three wins. It represents one added container and contributes nothing to the coin sum. Reveal it in the ledger's normal row sequence with a dedicated registered cue, initially `reward.present`, with an existing appropriate placeholder until audio is produced. That cue belongs to the configured reward entry; generic code dispatches uniformly. Do not use unwrapping whistles to imply the hidden contents were revealed at reward time. The next action appears after the entire ledger, including this row, finishes presenting, consistent with the existing result-menu decision.

The model commits reward commands, owned card, fixed payload, RNG position and paid-stage marker atomically, before the ledger animation. Store the finalized mixed entries so reopening or Continue presents the same result without generating, adopting or paying again. Existing cash balance validation remains meaningful: sum only financial effects, while grant records validate their own schema/references. A granted object moving later does not invalidate the historical reward receipt or authorize another grant. Audio/row callbacks only present the saved result; they do not create the Present. Every successful new boss-stage settlement gets the cue during normal presentation; replay/cleanup behavior follows the presentation scheduler rather than mutating the award.
